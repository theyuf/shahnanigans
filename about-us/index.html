WHO ARE WE?

Shahnanigans is a boutique creative studio based in West London, UK. 

Established in 2019, our portfolio boasts collaborations with globally renowned brands, delivering over 50 innovative AR experiences. With an impressive track record, we have generated a staggering 40 billion+ views across various platforms for our clients.

We specialise in crafting distinctive marketing solutions through augmented reality, digital storytelling and immersive experiences. We bring a thoughtful and effective approach to enhance your brand presence.

Explore the possibilities of creative and innovative marketing with us.

Meet Shahwali Shayan, also known as Shah. In 2019, he founded Shahanigans to blend his passions for interactive design, art, and digital marketing, and time has flown since!

Shahanigans commenced its journey by constructing interactive and captivating experiences on social media using Augmented Reality filters. However, the expansion of AR has permeated numerous facets of marketing, ranging from packaging enhancements to location-based activations, showcasing its ever-evolving nature.

As both a graduate and now a guest lecturer at the Royal College of Arts MA in Digital Direction program, Shah's research centers around the narrative potential of interactive experiences employing cutting-edge technology. He expresses tremendous excitement about Web 3.0, finding the transition exceptionally captivating.
