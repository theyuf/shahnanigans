How do you connect with your audience? Is there a way to interact with your audience on social media in a fun new way? 

It is becoming more difficult to market your brands on social media as the attention span of online audiences is going down. Traditional digital ads get lost in the clutter online and people seem to scroll past them. Tiktok filters and Snapchat Lenses, also known as Augmented reality (AR) experiences can change that!

How do Tiktok filters and Snapchat Lenses change that?

Augmented reality filters allow you to interact with your audience and build loyal communities. It is easier to promote and communicate with your audience using filters as the audience gets to participate in your campaign.This fosters a sense of collective involvement through individualised experiences, translating into tangible results.

And numbers don’t lie!

What are the benefits of leveraging AR filters?

A filter that is developed and executed creatively has the potential to go viral leading to a higher number of impressions and a lower cost per user. Your users engage with your brand leading to more exposure and traffic when they create content using your filters.
